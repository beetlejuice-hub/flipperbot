# Sneakers: what moves the price, what to look for, what to avoid

Flipping sneakers bought in Romania (OLX, Publi24, Facebook). Model, colourway, size and condition set the price;
fakes are everywhere.

## Cheat sheet (the short version the quick AI read gets)

- Hype models: Jordan 1 High/Low, Jordan 4, Nike Dunk Low, Air Max 1/90/95, Yeezy 350, adidas Samba/Gazelle/Spezial,
  New Balance 550/2002R/9060/990, Asics Gel-Kayano 14/1130, Salomon XT-6, Onitsuka Mexico 66.
- Sizes EU 41-45 sell fastest; very small and very large sizes slowly.
- New with box and tags (DS) beats worn; creasing, sole wear, yellowing and missing box cut value hard.
- Fakes: "calitate premium", "1:1", "UA", "top quality", "first copy", many sizes of the same pair from one seller, a
  price at a third of StockX. Check the size-tag style code matches the colourway, stitching, box label vs shoe.
- Market in 2026: overall resale cooled (same shoes ~7% cheaper than a year before); Saucony, Vans, Asics and
  adidas terrace models rose. Price against recent sold prices (StockX/GOAT/eBay sold), not hype.
- Low resale: generic "adidasi" without a model, fashion collabs nobody searches, kids' sizes.
- Designer pairs (the most faked of all): Rick Owens Geobasket, Ramones, Jumbo Laces, Dunks; Maison Margiela GAT
  ("Replica" is Margiela's real line name, not a confession) and Tabi; Balenciaga Triple S, Track, Speed, 3XL;
  Alexander McQueen oversized; Golden Goose Superstar; Common Projects Achilles; Off-White Out of Office, ODSY;
  Dior B22/B23/B30; Amiri Skel, MA-1; Prada America's Cup; Louis Vuitton Trainer. A new pair at a few hundred lei
  is a replica; worn genuine pairs still fetch ~40-60% of retail.
- Replica sellers in Romania: "premium", "calitate premium", "super calitate", "full box", every size listed
  ("40 41 42 43 44", "39-45"), "livrare gratuita, plata ramburs", many hype pairs new from one seller.

## Fake tells

- Nike/Jordan: the size tag's style code (e.g. DD1391-100) must match the colourway (search it); the box label
  matches the tag; stitching even, no glue; Jordan 4 netting angle and tongue tag; Dunk Low toe box and swoosh
  shape; "UA" (unauthorized authentic) is a replica word.
- Yeezy: the boost visible through the sole, the heel tab and pull loop, the size-tag codes.
- Rick Owens: made in Italy, thick leather, the inner size label and "Rick Owens" debossed insole, heavy
  pentagram stitched sole on Geobaskets.
- Margiela GAT: numbers 22 tag stitched at the heel, made in Italy (or Portugal for some), suede toe texture.
- Balenciaga Triple S: size printed at the toe, laser-etched sizing on the tongue side, made in Italy (early) or
  China (later, genuine); fakes get the sole layers and the font wrong.
- Golden Goose: the hand-distressed look is uneven on purpose, star leather quality, "GGDB" inner label and serial.
- Ask for: a photo of the size tag inside the tongue, the box label, the soles, and the insole lifted out.
