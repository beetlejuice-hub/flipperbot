# Men's clothing: what resells, what's fake, what to pay

Flipping second-hand men's clothes bought in Romania (OLX, Facebook, Publi24, Lajumate; Vinted by hand) and
resold on Vinted, Grailed, Vestiaire or OLX. Brand, the exact line, condition, size and season set the price.
Fakes are the biggest risk: Romanian OLX men's clothing is full of replicas.

## Cheat sheet (the short version the quick AI read gets)

- Brand tiers by resale value:
  - grail / archive: Rick Owens (and DRKSHDW), Maison Margiela, Raf Simons, Helmut Lang (90s-2000s), Undercover,
    Number (N)ine, Chrome Hearts, Comme des Garcons, Yohji Yamamoto, Issey Miyake (Homme Plisse), Kapital, Visvim,
    Jean Paul Gaultier (archive mesh), Vivienne Westwood, Junya Watanabe, Sacai, Jil Sander, Dries Van Noten.
  - luxury outerwear and houses: Moncler, Canada Goose, Parajumpers, Prada (nylon, Linea Rossa), Burberry, Loro Piana,
    Brunello Cucinelli, Balenciaga, Gucci, Dior, Louis Vuitton, Saint Laurent, Off-White, Amiri.
  - premium (the reseller's bread and butter): Stone Island, C.P. Company, Arc'teryx (and Veilance), Patagonia,
    The North Face (Nuptse, Himalayan, Summit), Barbour, Carhartt WIP and workwear Carhartt, Stussy, Supreme, Palace,
    Our Legacy, Acne Studios, Aime Leon Dore, Fear of God / Essentials, Corteiz, Trapstar, Represent, Kith, Needles,
    Engineered Garments, Nanamica, And Wander, Snow Peak, Salomon, Nike ACG, Ralph Lauren RRL, Schott, Belstaff.
  - basic (sells, for little): Polo Ralph Lauren, Lacoste, Tommy Hilfiger, Levi's (except vintage 501/LVC/Big E),
    Nike/Adidas sportswear, Hugo Boss, Gant, Fred Perry, Napapijri, Columbia, Calvin Klein, Armani.
  - no value: Zara, H&M, Pull&Bear, Bershka, Reserved, LC Waikiki, Shein, Primark, Jack & Jones, Cropp, House.
- Lines that matter more than the brand: Arc'teryx Alpha SV / Beta LT / Beta AR / Atom (Gore-Tex Pro shells hold
  value); TNF Nuptse 1996 / 700 fill, Himalayan, Steep Tech, Summit Series; Moncler Maya, Montclar, Karakorum,
  Grenoble; Canada Goose Expedition, Chilliwack, Wyndham, Langford; Patagonia Retro-X, Snap-T, Synchilla, Nano
  Puff, Das Parka; Barbour Bedale, Beaufort, International; Carhartt Detroit, OG Active, Michigan, Double Knee;
  Stone Island Ghost, Shadow Project, Ice Jacket, Membrana, Crinkle Reps, Nylon Metal; C.P. Company Goggle,
  Mille Miglia; Supreme Box Logo; Ralph Lauren Polo Bear sweaters, vintage Polo Sport / Polo Country; Levi's
  vintage 501 (made in USA, red tab, Big E), Type I-III truckers; Nike Tech Fleece (current), vintage Nike ACG.
- Sleepers (sellers don't know what they have): old Stone Island and C.P. Company (Massimo Osti era, 80s-90s);
  Helmut Lang, Raf Simons and Margiela archive mixed in a "lot haine"; Arc'teryx sold as "geaca de munte"
  (mountain jacket); Patagonia fleeces at a thrift price; Japanese labels (Kapital, Needles, Visvim, Engineered
  Garments, Beams Plus, Porter) that Romanian sellers don't recognise; Barbour waxed jackets sold as "geaca de
  vanatoare" (hunting jacket); military surplus (M-65, original Alpha MA-1) sold for little; vintage band and
  rap tees (single stitch, 90s); Chrome Hearts silver sold as "inel argint" (silver ring).
- What sells fastest: outerwear in October-January, M and L sizes (S and XXL+ slower), black and navy, clean
  logos. Tees and basics sell slowly unless hype or vintage.
- Condition: new with tags (cu eticheta) adds a lot; pilling, stains, holes, broken zips, worn cuffs and collars,
  missing hood or fur trim cut value hard. A washed-out down jacket loses loft.
- Fakes: "premium", "super calitate", "calitate 1:1", "calitate turceasca" (Turkish quality), "colectie noua",
  every size in stock, delivery and cash on delivery, the brand name split or disguised ("mo n cler",
  "b@lenciaga", "g.u.c.c.i"), stock photos, many "luxury" pieces from one seller, a price under a third of
  second-hand value. Check below per brand.

## Fake tells per brand

- Stone Island: the Compass badge is embroidered (not printed), with a textured feel and two buttons at the top on
  the sleeve; the back of the badge has a white label with its own serial, the inner label has the CE label and a
  Certilogo code (check it at certilogo.com: a fake code fails or is reused); the care-label number matches the
  badge; the Ghost pieces have a tone-on-tone badge. Fakes: badge colours off, wrong font, buttons that don't fit,
  no serials, "made in China" or Turkey.
- C.P. Company: Goggle lenses metal-ringed, correct lens tint, the CP logo on the sleeve lens, Certilogo or a QR tag
  on newer pieces; fakes have plastic lenses, wrong placement.
- Moncler: after 2013 a white tag with an NFC chip and QR code (check on code.moncler.com), the rooster logo on
  the felt badge with fine stitching, Lampo or Riri zips, a hologram label (older), the down tag stating feather
  content. Fakes: badge with thick lettering, the cock's face blurred, cheap YKK copies, no NFC tag.
- Canada Goose: the Arctic disc patch with thin, crisp leaves and lettering, a hologram label with a polar bear
  on newer jackets, YKK Vislon zips, real coyote fur (now often none: post-2022 ones have no fur), the "made in
  Canada" label. Fakes: thick lettering on the patch, faux fur, wrong zip pulls.
- Arc'teryx: the bird logo embroidered with fine, dense stitches; Gore-Tex label inside; YKK AquaGuard zips;
  the product label with model and serial; laser-cut, taped seams. Fakes: thick embroidery, bad taping, a price
  for an Alpha SV under ~900 lei.
- The North Face: the logo embroidered clean (letters not touching), a hologram on newer labels, the Nuptse's 700
  fill tag, model codes on the inner label. Fakes: fat embroidery, wrong fill weight, "Nuptse" in all sizes and
  colours new.
- Supreme: Box Logo letters with clean, tight embroidery and correct spacing; neck and wash tags of the season;
  fakes are common at 100-300 lei.
- Chrome Hearts: heavy .925 silver with hallmarks and "CH" stamps, thick screen prints on tees with the
  Hollywood/Los Angeles back print correct; nearly all cheap ones are fake.
- Ralph Lauren: the pony embroidered with defined rider and polo stick (one blob = fake); Polo Bear sweaters
  have a knitted, not printed, bear.
- Lacoste: the croc has teeth and claws, a stitched outline; the size label has numbers (2-8), not S-XL, on
  classic polos.
- Off-White, Balenciaga, Gucci, LV, Dior, Amiri, Palm Angels, Dsquared2, Moncler tees and Hugo Boss "premium":
  the most copied in Romania; a new one at a shop-like listing is almost always a replica.
- Carhartt: the square logo patch stitched all round; WIP labels say "Carhartt WIP". Few fakes: mostly a
  question of the line and condition.
- Levi's vintage: a red tab with "LEVI'S" (Big E = before 1971), the button number (555 = Valencia Street,
  made in USA), single-stitch selvedge on old 501s.

## Prices (second-hand, Romania and EU resale, lei)

Rough, for a checked, good-condition piece; the price book learns the real ones:
- Moncler puffer 1,800-4,500; Canada Goose parka 2,000-4,500; Stone Island jacket 800-2,500, hoodie/sweatshirt
  350-700, overshirt 600-1,200; C.P. Company Goggle jacket 800-1,800; Arc'teryx Alpha SV 2,000-3,500, Beta LT
  1,200-2,000, Atom 500-900; TNF Nuptse 1996 500-1,000; Barbour Bedale/Beaufort 400-900; Patagonia Retro-X
  400-700; Carhartt Detroit 400-900, Double Knee 250-450; Supreme Box Logo hoodie 1,200-3,000; Polo Bear sweater
  400-900; vintage Levi's 501 made in USA 200-500.
- Reselling costs: Vinted charges the buyer, not the seller; Grailed ~9-10% + payment; Vestiaire ~15-25%; OLX
  free (promoted listings cost). Shipping 15-25 lei in Romania.

## Where to sell

Vinted (largest second-hand clothing market in Romania and Europe, buyer pays the fee); Grailed for archive and
designer; Vestiaire Collective for luxury (it authenticates); OLX for outerwear locally.
