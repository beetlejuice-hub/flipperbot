# LEGO: what moves the price, what to look for, what to avoid

Flipping LEGO bought in Romania. The set number decides everything; sealed retired sets are worth the most.

## Cheat sheet (the short version the quick AI read gets)

- Price by set number (on the box, instructions or listing). Sealed (MISB) beats built; built complete with
  instructions and box beats built loose; incomplete sells by the kg.
- Retired sets rise (median roughly doubles over 5 years, not all of them). ~280 sets retire end of December 2026,
  e.g. UCS Millennium Falcon 75192, Technic Ford GT 42154, Koenigsegg Jesko 42173, Himeji Castle 21060.
- Strong themes: Star Wars UCS, Icons/Creator Expert (modulars), Technic supercars, Ideas, Harry Potter, Architecture.
- Clones: Lepin, Mould King, Cada, "tip LEGO", "compatibil LEGO": no LEGO logo on the studs, worth little.
- Minifigures sell separately and can carry a set's value (Star Wars, Harry Potter exclusives).
- Bulk ("kg LEGO", "lot LEGO") from a parent's attic can hide old sets and minifigures: sort and part them out.
- Prices: BrickLink (sold), BrickEconomy (value and growth), Brickfact (retirement dates).
