# Phones: what moves the price, what to look for, what to avoid

Flipping second-hand phones bought in Romania (OLX, Publi24, Facebook). Price by exact model AND storage.

## Cheat sheet (the short version the quick AI read gets)

- iPhones hold value best, then Samsung Galaxy S/Z, then Pixels; other makers resell for little.
- Price = model x storage x condition x battery health (iPhones: 85%+ sells normally, under 80% needs a battery).
- Deal-breakers: iCloud/Activation Lock, Google FRP lock, "codat in retea" (network-locked), blacklisted or reported
  IMEI, "Android clone" of an iPhone. These are not resellable as working phones.
- Lower value: replaced non-original screen ("display neoriginal", True Tone missing), Face ID not working,
  refurbished sold as new, deep scratches, swollen battery.
- Fast sellers: iPhone 13-16 (Pro), Galaxy S23/S24, Z Flip; slow: old Androids, small-storage models.
- Red flags: far under market, "full box sigilat" at half price, stock photos, courier-only, many identical phones.

## Reselling tips

- Check IMEI (imei.info, carrier blacklist), battery health screenshot, iCloud signed out in front of you.
- Clean, photograph the screen on, the settings "About" page, battery health; sell with box and receipt if you have them.
