# Bicycles: buy tired, service, wash, sell ready to ride

Flipping second-hand bicycles bought in Romania (OLX, Facebook), with a budget of about 1,500 lei a bike. The money
is in decent brand-name bikes that look neglected (flat tyres, rusty chain, dirty, "a stat in garaj"), bought in
autumn and winter, serviced and cleaned, and sold in spring.

## Cheat sheet (the short version the quick AI read gets)

- What sells: adult hardtail MTBs 27.5"/29" from known brands (Trek Marlin/X-Caliber, Specialized Rockhopper,
  Cube Aim/Attention/Analog, Scott Aspect/Scale, Giant Talon, Merida Big Nine/Big Seven, Kellys, Author, Rockrider
  ST 520/540/900), road and gravel bikes (Triban RC 120/520, Van Rysel, Giant Contend, Specialized Allez, Trek
  Domane), city/trekking bikes for commuting, kids' bikes 20"/24" (sell fast before spring and Christmas), and
  Brompton folding bikes (hold value very well).
- Price drivers: brand and line, the groupset (Shimano Deore/SLX/XT and SRAM NX/GX on MTBs; Shimano 105/Ultegra on
  road bikes add a lot; Tourney/Altus/Claris are entry level), hydraulic disc brakes, an air suspension fork
  (RockShox, Fox, Manitou) vs a basic spring fork (SR Suntour XCT), frame material (carbon > aluminium > heavy steel),
  wheel size (26" MTBs are dated and cheaper), frame size (M and L sell fastest; XS and XXL slowly), and condition.
- Low value: supermarket bikes (DHS, Velors, Carpat, Kands, Devron, no-name "full suspension" for 600 lei new),
  26" department-store MTBs, rusted steel city bikes, exercise bikes (not bikes).
- Sleepers: an old Trek, Cannondale, Specialized or GT from the 2000s with quality parts listed as "bicicleta veche";
  vintage steel road bikes (Peugeot, Raleigh, Bianchi, Colnago, Favorit, old Pegas/Tohan) for the vintage and fixie
  crowd; Pegas Practic/Strada (the revived Romanian brand, sought after); a Brompton listed as "bicicleta pliabila";
  quality parts on a cheap bike (a Deore groupset, an air fork) worth more than the bike.
- The flip: a full service (clean, degrease, new chain if stretched, tyres/tubes, brake pads, cable adjust, true the
  wheels) costs 100-300 lei in parts if you do it yourself; a good wash and photos in daylight add a lot; accessories
  that help it sell: mudguards, a kickstand, lights, a bottle cage, new grips. Sell in March-June at the top.
- Stolen bikes: a premium bike far under market, a seller who can't say the frame size or the groupset, "fara acte"
  / "fara factura", a new OLX account, photos at night or at a block entrance. Ask for the invoice (factura) and the
  frame serial number (under the bottom bracket) and check it (bikeindex.org, local police posts). Never buy a bike
  that looks stolen.
- Faults: a cracked or bent frame is not a flip (aluminium can't be safely repaired); an e-bike with a dead battery
  needs a 1,000-2,500 lei battery; a seized suspension fork or a worn drivetrain (chain, cassette, chainrings) eats
  the margin; a flat tyre, a rusty chain, out-of-tune gears and worn brake pads are the cheap ones.

## Prices (second-hand, Romania, lei, good condition)

Rough, for a sense of scale; the price book learns the real ones: Rockrider ST 520 600-900, ST 540 900-1,400;
Triban RC 120 800-1,200, RC 520 1,500-2,200; Trek Marlin 5 1,100-1,600, Marlin 7 1,800-2,600; Specialized Rockhopper
1,200-2,200; Cube Aim 1,000-1,600, Attention 1,500-2,300; Scott Aspect 900-1,600; Merida Big Nine 1,000-2,000;
Kellys Spider/TNT 800-1,500; kids' 20"/24" brand bikes 300-800; vintage steel road bikes 300-1,200; Brompton
3,500-6,000. Supermarket bikes 150-500.

## Where to sell

OLX (most buyers, local pick-up), Facebook groups and Marketplace, Vinted for kids' bikes and accessories. Spring
and early summer are the season; a clean bike with fresh tyres and good photos sells in days.
