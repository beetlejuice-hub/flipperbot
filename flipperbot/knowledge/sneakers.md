# Sneakers: what moves the price, what to look for, what to avoid

Flipping sneakers bought in Romania (OLX, Publi24, Facebook). Model, colourway, size and condition set the price;
fakes are everywhere.

## Cheat sheet (the short version the quick AI read gets)

- Hype models: Jordan 1 High/Low, Jordan 4, Nike Dunk Low, Air Max 1/90/95, Yeezy 350, adidas Samba/Gazelle/Spezial,
  New Balance 550/2002R/9060/990, Asics Gel-Kayano 14/1130, Salomon XT-6, Onitsuka Mexico 66.
- Sizes EU 41-45 sell fastest; very small and very large sizes slowly.
- New with box and tags (DS) beats worn; creasing, sole wear, yellowing and missing box cut value hard.
- Fakes: "calitate premium", "1:1", "UA", "top quality", "first copy", many sizes of the same pair from one seller, a
  price at a third of StockX. Check the size-tag style code matches the colourway, stitching, box label vs shoe.
- Market in 2026: overall resale cooled (same shoes ~7% cheaper than a year before); Saucony, Vans, Asics and
  adidas terrace models rose. Price against recent sold prices (StockX/GOAT/eBay sold), not hype.
- Low resale: generic "adidasi" without a model, fashion collabs nobody searches, kids' sizes.
